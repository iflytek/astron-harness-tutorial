# AStudio Introduction

> Source: [Official AStudio documentation](https://agent.xfyun.cn/studio-guide/intro) · Checked: 2026-10-27. Product behavior and availability may change; consult the current official page.

## What is AStudio?

AStudio is an AI productivity workspace. Give it a goal and supporting material to plan work, use tools and deliver documents, spreadsheets, websites, images or code. Review the result in the preview panel, then refine it through conversation or targeted edits.
