## Apple company research

Select company research in the financial research plugin. Enter AAPL, a cutoff date and the purpose of the report. In the Word document, trace one revenue figure to the annual filing and check its period and currency.

## Find studies on fast charging and battery capacity loss

In the academic research plugin, select paper search and specify years, cell type and inclusion criteria. Follow a DOI from the results and check whether the experiment measured capacity change under the specified charging conditions.

## Read a paper alongside its PDF

Upload a complete PDF with selectable text to the paper-reading tool. Specify your background and request a two-column HTML reading page. Follow a source tag to the highlighted passage and compare a formula’s subscripts with the original.
