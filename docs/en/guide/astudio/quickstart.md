Use the download entry on the [official quick-start page](https://agent.xfyun.cn/studio-guide/quickstart). Supported environments listed there are Apple silicon Macs and 64-bit Windows 10 / 11. Install the client and sign in.

## Complete your first task

1. Select Daily in the left navigation and create a task.
2. Describe the desired result in the center. Attach or drag in reference files.
3. Send the request and inspect the result in the right panel.
4. Request specific changes and check the final output.

Practice with your own meeting notes: “Create an action list with owners, deadlines and unresolved questions.”

## Find your way around

The left panel manages tasks, projects and settings; the center contains conversation and execution; the right panel previews files, websites, changes and the terminal. Continue to [projects and task modes](./features) for ongoing folder-based work. See [plans](./pricing) for point validity.
