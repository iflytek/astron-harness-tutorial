# AStudio Plans & Points

> Source: [Official AStudio documentation](https://agent.xfyun.cn/studio-guide/pricing) · Checked: 2026-10-26. Product behavior and availability may change; consult the current official page.

## Compare plans

AStudio offers Trial, Standard and Advanced plans with different point allowances, daily rewards, top-up discounts and resource benefits. One-month, recurring monthly and recurring annual billing have different prices.

Compare the [current official table](https://agent.xfyun.cn/studio-guide/pricing) and confirm the checkout amount, period and promotion conditions.

## Point validity

| Category | Current rule |
| --- | --- |
| New-user reward | 1,000 points, valid for 3 months |
| Daily reward | 31 days after collection |
| Plan allowance | Current billing cycle; annual plans refresh monthly |
| Purchased top-ups | Current membership billing month |

Check the point center for exact expiry. Model and paid API usage consume points. Earliest-expiring points are used first; equal expiry prioritizes rewards, plan allowance, then top-ups.

## Before subscribing

Check renewal status and the next charge. The current guide does not support mid-cycle tier changes; cancellation retains paid benefits until expiry. Read the [subscription](./agreements/product-subscription-service) and [renewal](./agreements/auto-renewal-service) terms.
